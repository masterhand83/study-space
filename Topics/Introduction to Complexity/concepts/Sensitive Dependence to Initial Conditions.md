## In my own words

It is the study of systems whose final conditions change based on the state of the initial conditions.

## Real definition
Sensitive dependence on initial conditions is the property of certain dynamical systems, especially chaotic ones, whereby arbitrarily small differences in a system’s starting state grow, often exponentially, over time until the resulting trajectories become completely different from one another
