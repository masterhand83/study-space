# AWS Certified AI Practitioner (AIF-C01)

> [!important] Booking rule
> Don't book by date. **Book the exam once you score 80% or more on two practice exams.** People learn at different speeds, and this rule adjusts to yours. If that happens in November, book November.

**Exam basics** (check before booking, AWS updates these): about 65 questions, 90 min, passing score 700/1000. Question types include multiple choice and multiple response.
Official guide: <https://docs.aws.amazon.com/aws-certification/latest/examguides/ai-practitioner-01.html>
Free prep: AWS Skill Builder's *Exam Prep Plan* and *Official Practice Question Set* for AIF-C01.

### Domain 2: Fundamentals of Generative AI (24%)

### Domain 5: Security, Compliance, and Governance for AI (14%)
- [ ] 5.1 Securing AI systems
- [ ] 5.2 Governance and compliance for AI systems

## High-value note to build: "Which service?"

Many exam questions come down to **picking the right AWS service for a scenario**. Studying similar services side by side (interleaving) helps you tell them apart. Build one note, `Which AWS AI service.md`, with a row per service: *what it's for · a typical scenario · what it gets confused with*. Then ask an AI for `exam which-service`.
